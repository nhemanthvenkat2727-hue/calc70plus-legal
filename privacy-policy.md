# Privacy Policy for Calc70plus

**Last updated: 18 November 2026**

Calc70plus ("the app," "we," "us") is developed by NALLABOTHULA HEMANTH VENKAT. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

## The short version

Calc70plus has no account system and no server of ours. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location. Everything the app remembers — your favourites, calculation history, streaks, reminders, and settings — is stored only on your own device.

To keep the app free and stable, two Google services process limited technical data: **Google AdMob** (advertising) and **Firebase Crashlytics** (crash reports). Both are described below.

## What the app stores on your device

All of the following is stored locally on your device only, using Android's standard app storage. We never receive a copy:

- Favourite and recently used calculators
- Calculation history (so you can revisit past results)
- Test Mode drill results, streaks, questions-answered totals, and practice statistics
- Bookmarked Tricks & Shortcuts
- Study Reminders and study notes you create, including any due times you choose
- App settings (theme, home screen layout, haptics, notification times, etc.)
- Whether you have purchased Calc70plus Pro, and which plan

If you uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings, this data is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered by us — because we never had a copy of it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Calc70plus shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Calc70plus Pro removes ads. Some ads are optional "rewarded" ads that you choose to watch in exchange for a benefit.

To show ads and measure how they perform, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which approximate location may be derived), device model, operating system version, and how you interact with ads. This information is processed by Google under [Google's own policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). We do not receive your personal data from AdMob; we only see aggregated reports about ad performance and earnings.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your phone's Ads settings (usually under Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before showing personalised ads.

## Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report to Google so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains technical details about the crash, your device model, operating system version, app version, and an anonymous installation identifier. It does not contain your name, email, or anything you type into the app. Crash data is handled under [Firebase's privacy information](https://firebase.firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and is used only to improve the app's stability.

## Notifications

If you turn on Study Reminders, the app schedules notifications directly through your device's operating system. Exam countdown, FD/RD maturity, streak, come-back, and study-note reminders are all scheduled and delivered entirely on your phone — no reminder content is sent to or through any server we operate.

## Purchases

If you purchase Calc70plus Pro, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details (card numbers, UPI details, etc.) — Google processes and secures that information under its own privacy policy, available at [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made, which the app stores locally on your device as described above.

## Currency converter

The in-app currency converter fetches current exchange rates from a public rates service (Frankfurter, api.frankfurter.app). The request contains no personal information. Like any internet request, the service can see your device's IP address.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Internet and network state** — used for the currency converter, ads, crash reports, and opening external links you tap (Play Store, email).
- **Notifications** — used only to deliver the Study Reminders you choose to turn on. You can decline this permission and still use the rest of the app normally; you just won't receive reminder notifications.
- **Vibration** — used for haptic feedback. You can turn it off in the app's Settings.
- **Advertising ID** — used by Google AdMob as described above. You can reset or remove it in your phone's Ads settings.

## Data sharing, retention, and your choices

We do not sell your personal data. Data processed by Google (AdMob and Crashlytics) is kept according to Google's own retention policies. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to look up or delete on request; to control data held by Google, use your phone's Ads settings or Google's account tools. If you have any questions, contact us at the address below.

## Children's privacy

Calc70plus is intended for general audiences, including students preparing for competitive exams. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians who have concerns can contact us at the address below.

## Changes to this policy

If we change what data the app handles (for example, by adding new analytics, ads, or account features), we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above, and — where required — notify you within the app.
